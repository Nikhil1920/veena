Spoken by about 90 million people across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, Telugu is the largest Dravidian language and one of the most widely spoken languages of India, with a presence that also reaches Yanam and diaspora communities around the world. Despite its scale, Telugu voice typing has long depended on cloud services that need internet access and upload your audio to remote servers. Veena changes that: **Telugu speech to text that runs entirely on your own device.**

## Telugu speech to text with Veena

Veena's recognition engine is built on IndicConformer, a multilingual model from AI4Bharat, with a dedicated vocabulary and decoding configuration for Telugu. Tap the microphone, speak in Telugu, and Veena converts your words into editable text — instantly and offline. The transcript appears in the Telugu script, ready to edit, copy, or share.

Telugu is also the one language in Veena with its own dedicated model. The app downloads it during setup, about 125 MB, while the other 21 languages share the multilingual model. On the desktop app, the same Telugu model is available alongside the other languages.

## Why transcribe Telugu audio

Telugu is a language of government, education, cinema, and business across two large states, and there are plenty of reasons to turn speech into text:

- **Office and administrative work.** Letters, applications, meeting notes, and reports in Telugu are faster to produce by speaking than by typing.
- **Education.** Students and teachers can dictate study notes, lesson summaries, and assignments in Telugu instead of laboriously typing them.
- **Journalism and media.** Reporters can transcribe interviews and field recordings in Telugu quickly, keeping quotes accurate without hours of manual typing.
- **Business communication.** Emails and messages to Telugu-speaking clients and colleagues flow faster when you can dictate them on the go.
- **Everyday messaging.** Long messages, lists, and notes in Telugu are easier to speak than to type on a phone keyboard.

## How to use Veena for Telugu

Open Veena, tap the microphone, and start speaking Telugu. The app records 16 kHz mono audio and transcribes it locally with the on-device model, then shows the transcript in an editable text box.

- On the **mobile app** (Android and iOS), use hold-to-record for short bursts or tap-to-record for longer dictation, then copy the transcript or share it.
- On the **desktop app** (Windows; macOS and Linux are in development), use the push-to-talk shortcut and the text is pasted directly into the app you are using, or copied to your clipboard.
- You can **replay the last recording** to check what was said, and every transcript can be edited before you copy it.

No internet connection is needed for transcription, and no account is required. Your audio never leaves your device.

## Tips for better accuracy in Telugu

The Telugu vocabulary in Veena is tuned for the language as it is spoken today across its main dialect regions, and a few habits make the transcript cleaner:

- **Speak your own Telugu.** Pronunciation and vocabulary differ between Andhra Pradesh and Telangana; the model is trained on data covering both regions, so speak the way you naturally do rather than trying to match a standard accent.
- **Give vowels their full length.** Telugu distinguishes short and long vowels, and vowel length can change a word's meaning. Let each vowel hold its natural length and the text follows.
- **Keep consonant clusters clear.** Telugu has clusters and combined consonants that blur when speech is rushed; a steady, natural pace keeps them distinct.
- **Speak in complete sentences.** The model performs best on natural, connected speech; very short isolated words can be harder to place without context.
- **Keep background noise low.** A quiet room matters more than a fancy microphone; the model works best when your voice is the clearest thing it hears.
- **Edit before you copy.** The transcript box is fully editable, so fix any slip before you paste the text into an email, document, or message.
