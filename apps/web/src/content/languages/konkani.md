Konkani, spoken by about 2.5 million people, is the official language of Goa and is also spoken along the coasts of Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Kerala. Few Indian languages are written in so many scripts. Devanagari is the official script, Roman script is widely used in Goa in newspapers and everyday writing, and the Kannada script is used by Konkani-speaking communities in coastal Karnataka. For a language with this kind of diversity, voice input has a special value: you speak, and the technology worries about the writing system. Veena changes that: **Konkani speech to text that runs entirely on your own device.**

## Konkani speech to text with Veena

Veena is a speech-to-text application for Indian languages, built on IndicConformer, a multilingual recognition model from AI4Bharat. That makes it easy to dictate in a public place or a busy office without worrying about who is listening to your recordings.

## Why transcribe Konkani audio

Goa's economy runs on tourism, hospitality, and small business, and Konkani is the language of daily life behind all of it. Transcribing spoken Konkani helps in practical ways.

Hoteliers, restaurateurs, and tour operators can dictate guest notes, menus, and itineraries in the language their staff actually speak. Local journalists and community papers, many of which publish in Roman-script Konkani, can capture interviews as text and edit them quickly. Students in Goa's schools, where Konkani is part of the curriculum, can dictate essays and practice material. Cultural groups documenting folk songs, tiatr theatre traditions, and family histories can turn recordings into searchable archives. Konkani communities in coastal Karnataka can keep records and correspondence in their own voice, even when their written practice uses yet another script.

Konkani is also the language of the family. Weddings, feasts, and community celebrations across Goa and the Konkan coast are conducted in it, and the letters, invitations, and announcements around those events are far easier to draft by voice than by typing. For the Goan and Konkani diaspora in other states and abroad, dictating a message in Konkani and sending it as text keeps the language alive across generations.

## Tips for better accuracy in Konkani

Konkani pronunciation varies noticeably between Goa and coastal Karnataka, and both are legitimate. Speak the way you normally do, and keep your style consistent within a recording. Speak at a steady pace and finish your words; slurred or rushed speech is harder for any recognizer. Record in a quiet space and keep the microphone close. Konkani vocabulary includes many words borrowed from Portuguese and Marathi; if a borrowed word is misheard, correct it in the transcript once, and keep speaking naturally rather than over-enunciating. Short recordings on a single topic tend to transcribe more reliably than long sessions that jump between subjects.
